# ZEN LAMP

A global question engine for reflection, awareness, and human growth.

ZEN LAMP is a simple but powerful idea:

One question can illuminate a human life.

The project combines philosophy, contemplative traditions, and AI to create a structured system of questions that help people reflect on themselves and their lives.

The name comes from the metaphor of a lamp.

A single lamp illuminates a person.
Many lamps illuminate humanity.

---

## Concept

Human beings have always used tools to understand themselves.

Religion, philosophy, and meditation were such tools.

ZEN LAMP is not a religion.

It is a **question system** designed to help people think.

AI acts as a mirror.
Humans bring meaning.

Together they create reflection.

---

## Structure

ZEN LAMP is built around three core elements.

### Daily Questions

365 questions designed for everyday reflection.

These questions help people pause and examine their lives.

### Strong Questions

100 deeper questions that confront core human themes:

- fear
- attachment
- loss
- meaning
- purpose

These are intended for deeper introspection.

### Gen and Wa

Two complementary AI response styles.

**Gen (玄)**  
Structural analysis.  
Clear, precise, confronting hidden assumptions.

**Wa (和)**  
Compassionate reflection.  
Gentle guidance and emotional resonance.

Together they create balance between insight and empathy.

## Purpose

ZEN LAMP is designed for people who feel:

- loneliness
- confusion
- emotional burden
- existential questions

Instead of giving answers, ZEN LAMP gives **questions**.

Because real understanding begins with a question.

The long-term vision is a global question platform where people can:

- encounter meaningful questions
- reflect on their lives
- contribute new questions
- explore human wisdom across cultures

A single lamp can illuminate a path.

Millions of lamps can illuminate humanity.
