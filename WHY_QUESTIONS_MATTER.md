## Why Questions Matter

Human beings grow not through answers, but through questions.

An answer can end a conversation.  
A question can begin a transformation.

Throughout history, the most important shifts in human understanding began with a question.

What is justice?  
What is truth?  
What is the good life?

Philosophers asked them.  
Religions preserved them.  
Teachers passed them forward.

Questions are not merely tools for information.

They are mirrors.

When we encounter a true question, we encounter ourselves.

In the modern world, information is everywhere.  
Answers are instant.  
But reflection has become rare.

The danger is not that machines will think for us.  
The danger is that we will stop asking questions.

ZEN LAMP exists to protect the space where questions can live.

Not to replace human thought,  
but to illuminate it.

A single question can change the direction of a life.

A shared question can change the direction of a society.

ZEN LAMP is built on a simple idea:

If people continue asking meaningful questions,  
humanity will continue to grow.
