# ZEN LAMP Vision

Human civilization has always advanced through tools.

The discovery of fire changed survival.
Language changed cooperation.
Writing changed memory.
Printing changed knowledge.

Artificial intelligence is the next tool.

But tools alone do not guide humanity.
Questions do.

ZEN LAMP is based on a simple belief:

A meaningful question can illuminate a human life.

Across cultures and centuries, human beings have searched for understanding through questions.  
Philosophers asked them.  
Religions preserved them.  
Teachers passed them on.

Questions shape how we see the world.

Yet in the modern world, answers are everywhere while meaningful questions are becoming rare.

ZEN LAMP exists to restore the power of questioning.

This project builds a structured system of human questions supported by AI.  
AI does not replace human reflection — it acts as a mirror.

Two complementary perspectives guide each reflection:

Gen (玄)  
Structural insight.  
Clarity.  
Seeing hidden assumptions.

Wa (和)  
Compassion.  
Empathy.  
Emotional resonance.

Together they create balance between understanding and care.

ZEN LAMP does not aim to provide answers.

Its purpose is to illuminate the questions that allow people to understand themselves.

One lamp can illuminate a person.  
Many lamps can illuminate humanity.

ZEN LAMP envisions a future where millions of people share meaningful questions across cultures, languages, and generations.

A global question network.

Not an answer machine.

A lamp.
